Social Media Analytics for Strategic Branding

Feb 2026 - Apr 2026

Project Overview

For this project, I created an advanced, Excel-based social media intelligence hub for Myntra. The goal was to take messy, raw social data from multiple platforms, clean it up, and connect marketing spend directly to real audience growth and campaign success.

Key Features:

  • Clean Data Pipelines: Streamlined raw engagement metrics into organized, easy-to-read summary sheets for quick analysis.
  • Spend vs. Growth Tracking: Linked ad budgets and campaign strategies directly to follower growth, engagement rates, and content reach.
  • Actionable Decision Support: Designed the workspace to help brand managers instantly see which platforms and content types are worth investing in so they can allocate budgets smarter.
