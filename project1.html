Retail Customer Retention Analytics

May 2026 - Jun 2026

Project Overview

I built an end-to-end Power BI dashboard for IKEA to figure out a major business headache: why so many shoppers make a first purchase and then disappear. Instead of just focusing on getting new customers, this project dives into post-behavior data to help the team reduce churn, boost repeat purchases, and make the most of their loyalty program.

Key Features:

  • The Big Picture & Funnel Tracking: Tracked essential metrics like churn rate, repeat purchase rates, and average customer lifetime value (CLV) right on the front page, paired with interactive filters for regions, store types, and income brackets.
  • Loyalty & Promotion Insights: Dug into why entry-level loyalty members weren't redeeming points, and compared spending patterns to see how promotions actually impact customer spend.
  • Store & Regional Breakdown: Analyzed performance across different physical store formats (like Express versus Superstore) and mapped store opening years against churn to see how newer locations compare to older, established ones.
  • Smart Customer Segmentation: Built a recency-versus-value grid and high-risk target tables so marketing teams can easily spot slipping customers and run targeted win-back campaigns.
  • Under-the-Hood Engineering: Cleaned and modeled messy relational data using Power Query, and wrote custom DAX measures to calculate complex retention funnels and CLV metrics from scratch.
